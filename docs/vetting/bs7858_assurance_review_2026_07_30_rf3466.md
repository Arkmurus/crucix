# BS 7858 assurance review — R-F3466

Date: 2026-07-30

Standard: **BS 7858:2019** (current at review date)

Product surface: `https://imaria.io/vetting.html`

## Truth boundary

BS 7858 is a code of practice for screening people working in secure
environments. It is not a certification generated by software and it is not
the whole recruitment process. A completed ARIA case supports a named human
controller's decision; it does not replace that decision, the employer's
accountability, a conformity audit, or a licensed copy of the standard.

The current-edition check was made against:

- BSI's product and history records for BS 7858:2019.
- SIA guidance on outsourced pre-employment screening, file-level audit and
  electronic-reference source verification.
- NSI Technical Bulletin 0049, which maps the 2019 revision and its process
  changes.
- NPSA employment-screening guidance for independent operational context.

No British Standard text is reproduced in the product or this review.

## What is now enforced

- One active product standard: BS 7858:2019. The create API rejects another
  framework identifier and the page has no standard/rule-pack selector.
- Historical internal versions remain resolvable only so signed case manifests
  can be replayed. They are not offered as alternative standards.
- Workflow order begins Interview, Application, Identity and right to work,
  Career history, Criminality and conduct, Public record and financial,
  Decision.
- The interview date is compared with the offer date. Missing proof is an
  action; an interview not earlier than the offer is a blocker.
- Every deterministic finding becomes a prioritised ARIA action with its
  reference, affected period and due date.
- Open cases refresh their assessment, documents and request ledger every
  30 seconds while visible, with an in-flight guard.
- Uploaded documents are encrypted, extracted, classified conservatively,
  linked to timeline periods only where relevant, and never auto-accepted
  when unreadable, low-confidence, authenticity-flagged or awaiting original
  sighting.
- Statutory declarations cannot bypass the timeline: the declaration document,
  named top-management approval and aggregate duration control are enforced.
- The clause register discloses partial, operator-controlled and unimplemented
  scope alongside encoded controls.

## Material go-to-market gaps

These prevent an honest claim that the software alone proves full conformity:

1. Organization-control register: top-management commitment, resources,
   assigned responsibilities and periodic working-practice review.
2. Screener competence register: initial training, annual competence review
   and retained training evidence.
3. Outsourcing governance: provider due diligence, contract controls and a
   named employing-organization review of every returned file.
4. Role/access scoping: identify ancillary staff and prove restricted access
   until screening is complete.
5. Acquisition/transfer workflow: review inherited files and initiate missing
   screening.
6. Named controller progress-review events. ARIA maintains the live progress
   ledger, but a human review event is not yet a first-class record.
7. Direct integrations for SIA-register and credit-reference searches. The
   module records these checks and their evidence; it does not perform them.
8. Independent legal/conformity review before marketing the service as
   “BS 7858 compliant” or certified. Product copy should say “supports
   screening to BS 7858:2019” until that review is complete.

## Assurance rule

No page, API, report or sales claim may convert `READY_FOR_CONTROLLER_REVIEW`
into “certified”, “passed” or “BS 7858 compliant”. Readiness means the encoded
case controls have no unresolved blocker/action under the pinned historical
manifest; organizational controls and the human decision remain separate.
