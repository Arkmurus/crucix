## Daily Compliance Questions to Ask ARIA

### Export Control & Licensing
```
ARIA, what are the export requirements for armoured vehicles to Angola?
What ML category do surveillance cameras fall under?
Do we need an ECJU licence to export body armour to Mozambique?
What's the ITAR status of products from [OEM name]?
Can we re-export US-origin components from the UK to Indonesia?
What are the dual-use considerations for thermal imaging equipment?
Is [product] caught by the Wassenaar Arrangement?
What's the current ECJU processing time for SITCL applications?
```

### Sanctions & Screening
```
/screen [company name]
/sanctions [person name]
ARIA, is [company] connected to any sanctioned entities?
What's the current sanctions landscape for Russia/Iran/North Korea?
Are there any new OFAC designations this week?
Has [entity] appeared on any watchlists recently?
```

### Country Risk & Market Assessment
```
/risk [country name]
ARIA, what are the arms embargoes affecting [country]?
What's the diversion risk for exports to [country]?
Compare the compliance risk of selling to Angola vs Indonesia
What offset requirements apply in Saudi Arabia?
What end-user certificate format does [country] require?
```

### Deal & Opportunity Analysis
```
ARIA, assess the opportunity for [product] in [country]
What's our relationship tier in [market]?
Who are our main competitors for [product] in [region]?
What procurement budget does [country] MoD have this year?
Is [country] currently running any tenders for [capability]?
```

### Teaching ARIA (Most Valuable)
```
/teach Angola FADM budget: The 2026 FADM budget is $3.2B, up 12%
/teach ECJU processing: Current standard SITCL processing is 8-12 weeks
/teach Indonesia offset: MEF programme requires 85% offset on contracts >$10M
/teach [country] MoD contact: General [Name] is the new procurement director
/teach Baykar pricing: TB2 export price is approximately $5M per unit with GCS
```

### Correcting ARIA (Most Valuable Training Signal)
```
/correct ARIA said Angola has no offset requirements -> Angola's SIMPORTEX manages offset obligations for major defence contracts, typically 15-30%
/correct You said ML4 covers ammunition -> ML4 covers bombs, torpedoes, rockets, missiles. Ammunition is ML3
/correct ITAR Category VII is not just vehicles -> ITAR Category VII covers vehicles, not vessels (Category VI covers vessels)
```

## How ARIA Gets Smarter Over Time

```
Week 1-2:  Team asks basic questions → ARIA builds foundational knowledge
Week 3-4:  Team corrects mistakes → ARIA eliminates errors
Month 2:   ARIA starts connecting patterns across conversations
Month 3:   Neural network matures — ARIA anticipates questions
Month 6:   ARIA proactively flags risks before you ask
Month 12:  Training data ready for fine-tuning a custom model
```

### The golden rules
1. **Ask, don't Google** — every question teaches ARIA
2. **Correct her when she's wrong** — `/correct` is the most valuable command
3. **Share everything** — documents, URLs, meeting notes, industry news
4. **Use Portuguese** for Lusophone work — she needs practice too
5. **Give feedback** — `/feedback +` or `/feedback -` helps her calibrate
6. **Forward emails** to aria@arkmurus.com — even newsletters help
7. **Tell her what happened** — "that deal was won/lost because..." trains her ML model
