R-F1512: increase seed signals for high-floor topics (>=70% need 5 signals)

Topics with hard floors of 70% (compliance, legal) need more than 2-3
signals to climb from 0.5 scaffold to above their floor. Now uses 5
signals for floor>=70%, 3 for below-floor, 2 for above-floor.
