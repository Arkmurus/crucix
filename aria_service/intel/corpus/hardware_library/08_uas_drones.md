# ARIA Military Hardware — Unmanned Aerial Systems
Source: Arkmurus Hardware Library v1.0 | Classification: ARKMURUS INTERNAL

## ARIA UAS Doctrine

Ukraine conflict has demonstrated drones are now decisive at every level. ARIA categorises:
- TIER 1: FPV attack drones — cheap, disposable ($400-2,000)
- TIER 2: Small ISR/attack (Switchblade, Lancet) — $10K-80K
- TIER 3: Tactical (Bayraktar TB2) — $1-5M
- TIER 4: MALE/HALE (MQ-9, Global Hawk) — $15M-$220M

## Combat-Proven Systems

### Bayraktar TB2 (Turkey — Baykar)
- Role: MALE tactical UCAV | Endurance: 27 hours | Ceiling: 25,000ft
- Armament: 4 x MAM-L/C smart micro munitions | Range: 150km line of sight
- Combat history: Libya (2020) — defeated Wagner-backed Haftar forces; Nagorno-Karabakh (2020) — decisive; Ukraine (2022) — effective early, degraded by EW/AD; Ethiopia, Sudan, Somalia
- Cost: ~$1-5M per unit (full system much more)
- Export: Turkey has supplied 30+ nations; no ITAR; Turkish export licence
- ARIA note: TB2 is the most important drone for African market BD — affordable, combat-proven, available without ITAR constraint. Burkina Faso, Mali, Ethiopia, Togo have procured/discussed.

### Shahed-136 / Geran-2 (Iran/Russia)
- Loitering munition / kamikaze drone | Range: 2,000km | Warhead: 50kg
- Cost: ~$20,000-50,000 per unit
- Combat history: Ukraine (2022-) — used by Russia in mass waves against Ukrainian cities and infrastructure
- ARIA note: Understanding Shahed capability essential for advising on air defence requirements against such threats

### MQ-9 Reaper (USA — General Atomics)
- MALE UCAV | Endurance: 27 hours | Ceiling: 50,000ft
- Armament: Hellfire missiles, GBU-12 Paveway, JDAM
- Cost: ~$30M | Export: ITAR controlled; very restricted
- ARIA note: Reaper availability to African states essentially zero through legitimate channels; creates alternative market

### Wing Loong II (China — AVIC)
- Chinese MALE UCAV equivalent to MQ-9 | Endurance: 26 hours
- Export: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Ethiopia, Nigeria, Pakistan, Jordan — broadly available without Western export constraints
- Cost: ~$1-2M
- ARIA note: China filling MALE gap left by US ITAR restrictions; Wing Loong proliferation to Africa is significant geopolitical trend

### HAROP (Israel — IAI)
- Loitering munition | 1,000km range | 23kg warhead
- Combat history: Azerbaijan (Nagorno-Karabakh 2020)
- Export: Israeli export licence; select nations only

### FPV Drones (Ukraine/Russia/China commercial)
- DJI-derived commercial quadcopters modified for grenade/bomb dropping
- Cost: $400-$2,000 per unit; Mavic-type ubiquitous
- Ukraine insight: Used in millions; primary ISR and improvised attack tool; $1,000 drones destroying $3M vehicles
- Export note: Commercial dual-use; China DJI origin triggers US security concerns
