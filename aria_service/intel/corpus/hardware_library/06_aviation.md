## Multirole Combat Aircraft — Generation Comparison

### Generation Framework
- Gen 3: MiG-21, F-4 Phantom — basic supersonic, no BVR
- Gen 4: F-16, MiG-29, Su-27 — BVR missiles, advanced avionics
- Gen 4.5: F-16V, Rafale, Eurofighter — AESA radar, advanced EW, BVR+
- Gen 5: F-22, F-35, Su-57 — low observability (stealth), sensor fusion
- Gen 6: Under development (USA, UK, Japan, EU, China)

### Su-27/30/35 Family (Russia)
- Su-27 Flanker (1985): Air superiority; exceptional manoeuvrability
- Su-30MKI/MKK: Two-seat multirole; thrust vectoring; widely exported
- Su-35S: 4++ generation; IRBIS-E PESA radar; superior BVR capability
- Export nations: India (Su-30MKI — 260+), China, Algeria, Vietnam, Indonesia, Angola (Su-30K)
- CPLP relevance: Angola operates Su-27/30 variants — a key market for maintenance, upgrade advisory, pilot training BD
- Export control: Russian state export (Rosoboronexport); currently under broad Western sanctions

### F-16 Fighting Falcon (USA — 1978, variants to F-16V Block 70/72)
- F-16V: AESA radar, upgraded avionics, EW suite
- Combat history: Israel-Arab Wars, Gulf War, Iraq, Afghanistan, Serbia, Libya, Syria, Ukraine (F-16s transferred 2024)
- Export: Widest-exported Western combat aircraft; 25+ nations; ITAR controlled; FMS standard
- Cost: F-16V Block 70: ~$62M per unit

### Rafale (France — 2001)
- Generation 4.5 | Multirole: air-to-air + deep strike + recce + nuclear
- AESA RBE2-AA radar | Spectra EW suite (considered world-class)
- Cost: ~$95-110M per unit (F3R standard)
- Recent deals: India (36), Egypt (24), Greece (18), Croatia (14), Indonesia (42), UAE (80), Serbia (12)
- ARIA note: France aggressively marketing Rafale; relevant for Portuguese-speaking market BD as France and CPLP overlap in Africa

### Eurofighter Typhoon (UK/Germany/Italy/Spain — 2003)
- Generation 4.5 | CAPTOR-E AESA radar (upgrade ongoing)
- Cost: ~$90-125M per unit
- ARIA note: UK-manufactured component; SITCL applies to brokering from UK perspective

### F-35 Lightning II (USA — 2015)
- Generation 5 | Low observability, sensor fusion, DAS all-aspect awareness
- Cost: F-35A ~$77.9M (2024 Lot 17)
- Export: ITAR controlled; JSF partner nations only
- ARIA note: Most politically sensitive arms export the US manages; non-partner nation access is restricted

## Light Attack & Counterinsurgency Aircraft

### A-29 Super Tucano (Brazil/USA — Embraer/Sierra Nevada)
- Role: Light attack, COIN, armed ISR trainer
- Cost: ~$9-14M per unit
- Combat history: Brazil, Afghanistan, Colombia, Burkina Faso, Nigeria, Senegal, Mauritania
- Export: Brazilian Embraer manufacture — no ITAR constraint on Brazilian-origin aircraft (critical market advantage)
- ARIA note: The most important light attack aircraft for African market BD — Brazilian manufacture avoids ITAR entirely. Angola, Mozambique, Cape Verde are all candidate markets.

### AT-6 Wolverine (USA — Beechcraft/Textron)
- Competitor to A-29 | ITAR controlled (US) | Cost: ~$12M

### L-39/L-159 ALCA (Czech Republic)
- Training + light attack | Czech manufacture — EU export rules | No ITAR constraint

### Yak-130 (Russia)
- Advanced jet trainer + light attack
- Angola operates Yak-130 — maintenance and upgrade opportunity

## Rotary Wing — Helicopters

### Mi-24/35 Hind (USSR/Russia — 1972)
- Role: Attack + troop transport hybrid (unique in world aviation)
- Armament: 12.7mm/23mm cannon; 57mm/80mm rockets; AT-6 Spiral ATGM | Transport: 8 troops
- CPLP relevance: Angola operated extensively during civil war; Mi-35M (modernised export) active market
- Cost: Mi-35M ~$12-18M

### Ka-52 Alligator (Russia — 2011)
- Coaxial rotor design | Combat history: Syria, Ukraine — performed poorly in early Ukraine operations (significant losses)
- Export: Egypt operates Ka-52E variant

### AH-64E Apache Guardian (USA — 1984, E-model 2011)
- Definitive Western attack helicopter | Target acquisition: Longbow radar + FLIR + Hellfire ATGM
- Cost: ~$35M | Export: ITAR controlled; major US FMS item

### EC725/H225M Caracal (France — Airbus Helicopters)
- Heavy tactical transport + SAR + special operations
- Brazil operates locally-built variant (H225M)
- ARIA note: Brazilian variant provides non-ITAR option for heavy tactical helicopter in CPLP market context
