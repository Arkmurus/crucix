# ARIA Military Hardware — Small Arms & Personal Weapons
Source: Arkmurus Hardware Library v1.0 | Classification: ARKMURUS INTERNAL

## ARIA Small Arms Doctrine

When comparing small arms, assess across seven dimensions:
1. Calibre and effective range
2. Rate of fire and magazine capacity
3. Weight and ergonomics (operator fatigue in prolonged operations)
4. Reliability in adverse conditions (sand, mud, extreme temperature)
5. Logistical footprint (ammunition commonality, spare parts availability)
6. Export control status (ITAR, SITCL, Wassenaar)
7. Combat-proven performance (verified conflict deployments)

## Assault Rifles — Generation by Generation

### AK-47 / AKM (USSR/Russia — 1947/1959)
- Calibre: 7.62x39mm | Effective range: 300-400m | ROF: 600 rpm
- Weight: 3.47kg (AKM unloaded) | Magazine: 30 rounds (standard)
- Reliability: Legendary — operates in extreme dust, mud, heat, cold
- Combat history: Korea, Vietnam, Angola, Mozambique, Gulf Wars, Syria, Ukraine, Sub-Saharan Africa conflicts — most widely deployed assault rifle in history; estimated 100M+ produced
- Export control: Widely proliferated; Russian variants ITAR-free; most African militaries operate some variant
- ARIA note: Standard comparison baseline for African market assessments. When a client asks what Angolan or Mozambican forces carry — AKM variants.

### AK-74 (USSR — 1974)
- Calibre: 5.45x39mm | Effective range: 500m | ROF: 650 rpm
- Weight: 3.3kg | Magazine: 30 rounds (5.45mm)
- Advantage over AKM: Higher velocity, flatter trajectory, less recoil, lighter ammunition load per soldier
- Combat history: Soviet-Afghan War, Chechen conflicts, Ukraine (2022-)
- ARIA note: Soviet successor to AKM; widely used in former Soviet sphere including some CPLP market forces.

### AK-12 (Russia — 2018)
- Calibre: 5.45x39mm | Effective range: 600m | ROF: 700 rpm
- Weight: 3.5kg | Magazine: 30 rounds (NATO STANAG compatible variants)
- Improvements: Folding/adjustable stock, full-length Picatinny rail, improved trigger, dust cover with rail, side-folding stock
- Combat history: Ukraine (2022-) — first major combat deployment
- Export status: Russian state arms export only (Rosoboronexport); currently under broad Western sanctions limiting legitimate transfer
- ARIA note: Russia's latest generation; relevant for understanding adversary capabilities in any Russian-influenced market.

### M16A4 / M4A1 (USA)
- Calibre: 5.56x45mm NATO | Effective range: 500m (M4A1 with 14.5" barrel)
- ROF: 800 rpm (M4A1 full auto) | Weight: 3.4kg (M4A1 unloaded) | Magazine: 30 rounds STANAG
- Reliability: Requires cleaner operating environment than AK; direct impingement gas system sensitive to carbon fouling
- Combat history: Gulf War, Iraq, Afghanistan, Syria (SOF), numerous partner force programmes globally
- Export status: ITAR controlled; FMS (Foreign Military Sales) required for government-to-government transfer; SITCL required for brokering
- ARIA note: Standard for NATO-aligned forces; any client seeking US-pattern weapons requires FMS compliance.

### HK G36 (Germany — 1997)
- Calibre: 5.56x45mm NATO | Effective range: 500m | ROF: 750 rpm
- Weight: 3.63kg | Magazine: 30 rounds STANAG compatible
- Reliability: Excellent in adverse conditions; gas-operated rotating bolt
- Controversy: Overheating accuracy degradation identified in Afghan operations — German Bundeswehr replacement initiated
- ARIA note: Widely exported but overheating issue affects long-burst sustained fire scenarios relevant to African operational environments.

### FN SCAR-L / SCAR-H (Belgium — 2009)
- SCAR-L: 5.56x45mm NATO | SCAR-H: 7.62x51mm NATO
- Effective range: 500m (L) / 800m (H) | ROF: 625 rpm
- Weight: 3.3kg (L) / 3.5kg (H) | Magazine: 30 rounds (L) / 20 rounds (H)
- Reliability: Short-stroke gas piston — cleaner operating than M16/M4
- Combat history: US SOCOM, Belgian Para-Commandos, SEAL Team 6
- Export status: Belgian/EU export licence required; ITAR applies to US-origin components
- ARIA note: Premium choice for SOF procurement; relevant for client forces building elite capability.

### SIG MCX Spear / M5 (USA — 2022)
- Calibre: 6.8x51mm SIG FURY (also known as .277 Fury)
- Effective range: 600m+ | ROF: 750 rpm | Weight: 3.6kg | Magazine: 20 rounds (hybrid steel/brass case)
- Significance: US Army NGSW winner; designed to defeat body armour at extended ranges; hybrid case achieves higher pressure than conventional brass
- Export status: ITAR controlled; not yet widely exported (2025)
- ARIA note: Represents next-generation capability; watch for international procurement interest post-US Army fielding.

## Sniper & Designated Marksman Rifles

### Dragunov SVD (USSR/Russia — 1963)
- Calibre: 7.62x54mmR | Effective range: 800m (1,300m maximum)
- Weight: 4.31kg | Magazine: 10 rounds
- Combat history: Every major Soviet/Russian conflict; widely exported to all former Soviet client states including Angola, Mozambique
- ARIA note: Baseline long-range capability in most CPLP African markets.

### Barrett M82/M107 (USA — 1989)
- Calibre: .50 BMG (12.7x99mm NATO) | Effective range: 1,800m
- Weight: 14kg | Magazine: 10 rounds
- Capability: Anti-materiel role — disabling vehicles, equipment, crew-served weapons; also used for explosive ordnance disposal
- Export status: ITAR controlled; broadly available via FMS

### AI AW / AXMC (UK — Various)
- Calibres: Multi-calibre platform — .308, .338 Lapua, .300 Win Mag
- Effective range: 1,100m (.338 Lapua) | Weight: 6.5kg
- ARIA note: UK-manufactured; SITCL may apply to brokering; considered the benchmark for precision sniper systems.

## Machine Guns — Squad & Vehicle Mounted

### PKM (USSR/Russia — 1969)
- Calibre: 7.62x54mmR | Effective range: 800m (1,000m with bipod)
- ROF: 650-750 rpm | Weight: 7.5kg | Feed: 100/200/250-round belt
- ARIA note: Most common belt-fed machine gun in CPLP African markets.

### M240B / FN MAG (USA/Belgium — 1977)
- Calibre: 7.62x51mm NATO | Effective range: 800-1,100m
- ROF: 650-900 rpm | Weight: 12.5kg | Feed: 100-round belt
- NATO standard: FN MAG adopted by UK (L7A2), USA (M240), Belgium, and dozens of NATO partners
- Export status: Belgian and US export licences required depending on variant

### M2 Browning .50 Cal (USA — 1933, still in service)
- Calibre: .50 BMG (12.7x99mm) | Effective range: 1,800m
- ROF: 450-600 rpm | Weight: 38kg (gun only)
- 90+ year service record; standard heavy machine gun for NATO and most Western-aligned forces worldwide
- ARIA note: Any African military modernisation programme will include M2 or equivalent (Russian DSHK/NSV) procurement.

### DSHK / NSV (USSR/Russia)
- Calibre: 12.7x108mm | Effective range: 2,000m
- Russian equivalent of M2; standard in former Soviet client states
- ARIA note: Common in Angola, Mozambique — important for ammunition logistics assessment in CPLP markets.
