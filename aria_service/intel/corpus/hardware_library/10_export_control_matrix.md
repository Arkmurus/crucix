# ARIA — Hardware Export Control Matrix
Source: Arkmurus Hardware Library v1.0 | Classification: ARKMURUS INTERNAL

## ARIA Compliance Rule

Every hardware procurement advisory must reference this matrix. Export control status determines whether Arkmurus needs a SITCL, whether the client needs end-user certification, and which suppliers can legitimately be approached.

## Wassenaar Arrangement Munitions List (ML) — Key Categories

- ML1: Small arms and light weapons (pistols, rifles, machine guns)
- ML2: Large calibre weapons (cannon >20mm, howitzers, mortars)
- ML3: Ammunition for ML1 and ML2 items
- ML4: Bombs, torpedoes, rockets, missiles, other explosive devices
- ML6: Ground vehicles and components (tanks, APCs)
- ML10: Aircraft, aero-engines, aircraft equipment
- ML11: Electronic equipment not elsewhere specified (EW, ECM)
- ML14: Specialised equipment for military training or simulators
- ML22: Technology for development/production of ML items

## US ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations) — Key Categories

- Category I: Firearms (ML1 equivalent)
- Category II: Guns and Armament (ML2 equivalent)
- Category III: Ammunition/Ordnance (ML3 equivalent)
- Category IV: Launch Vehicles, Guided Missiles, Ballistic Missiles
- Category VIII: Aircraft and Related Articles
- Category XI: Military Electronics
- Category XIII: Auxiliary Military Equipment

### ITAR Brokering Rule (22 CFR Part 129)
Any person who engages in the business of brokering activities for ITAR-controlled items must register with DDTC and may require a licence. This directly triggers UK SITCL requirement if Arkmurus is brokering ITAR-origin items.
