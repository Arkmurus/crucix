# ARIA Military Hardware — Naval Systems
Source: Arkmurus Hardware Library v1.0 | Classification: ARKMURUS INTERNAL

## Patrol Vessels & Maritime Security (African Market Focus)

### OPV (Offshore Patrol Vessel) 50-90m Class
- Most relevant vessel type for CPLP African markets
- Role: EEZ patrol, anti-piracy, fisheries protection, SAR
- Manufacturers: Damen (Netherlands), Naval Group (France), Lurssen (Germany), BAE Systems (UK)
- Cost range: $15-60M depending on specification

### Damen StanPatrol Series
- Most widely exported OPV family to Sub-Saharan Africa
- Ghana, Senegal, Cape Verde (vessels required), Mozambique
- ARIA note: Cape Verde maritime domain awareness is a consistent BD opportunity — island state requiring EEZ patrol

### Fast Patrol Craft / FPV (20-40m)
- Role: Coastal surveillance, anti-smuggling
- Cost: $3-12M | Short construction lead time
- Relevant suppliers: Damen, CMN (France), FB Design (Italy)

### Missile Corvette / Frigate
- Most capable surface combatant for African navies
- Angola is largest African naval power aspirant
- Cost: $200M-$800M per platform
- Current Angola procurement: Russian Buyan-class corvette discussion
- ARIA note: Any major naval procurement by CPLP African states involves significant G2G diplomacy

## Anti-Ship Missiles (Context for Naval BD)

### Exocet (France — MBDA)
- Range: 50-180km (variants) | Sea-skimming; radar-guided
- Combat history: Falklands War (1982 — sank HMS Sheffield); Iraq-Iran War
- Export: French/EU licence; widely exported (40+ nations)

### Harpoon (USA — Boeing)
- Range: 280km (Block II) | Active radar seeker; sea-skimming
- Export: ITAR controlled; most widely deployed Western anti-ship missile; 30+ operators

### C-802 / YJ-12 (China — CASIC)
- Range: 120-400km (variants)
- Proliferated to Iran, Pakistan, Bangladesh, various African states
- No ITAR/Wassenaar restriction on Chinese export — geopolitical tool
