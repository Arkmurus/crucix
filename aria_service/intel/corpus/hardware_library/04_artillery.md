# ARIA Military Hardware — Artillery & Indirect Fire
Source: Arkmurus Hardware Library v1.0 | Classification: ARKMURUS INTERNAL

## ARIA Artillery Doctrine

Artillery delivers 60-80% of all casualties in peer conflict (Ukraine data). ARIA assesses:
1. Range (determines safe distance from friendly forces)
2. Rate of fire (fire missions per hour sustained)
3. Accuracy (CEP without/with guidance)
4. Mobility (time from march to fire — critical for counter-battery)
5. Digital integration (fire control, targeting, BMS compatibility)
6. Ammunition commonality (interoperability with allies)

## Self-Propelled Howitzers

### M109A7 Paladin (USA)
- Calibre: 155mm/39 calibre | Range: 22km (HE) / 30km (RAP) / 40km (Excalibur)
- ROF: 4 rpm max / 1 rpm sustained | Crew: 4 | Weight: 36.7 tonnes
- Digital: AFATDS integrated — fully networked fire control
- Cost: ~$7M | Export: ITAR controlled; major FMS item

### PzH 2000 (Germany)
- Calibre: 155mm/52 calibre (longer = more range)
- Range: 30km (HE) / 40km (RAP) / 56km (Vulcano guided)
- ROF: 3 rounds in 9 seconds (burst) / 10-13 rpm sustained | Weight: 55.3 tonnes | Crew: 5
- Considered among best SPHs in service | Cost: ~$8-10M

### Caesar (France — wheeled SPH)
- Calibre: 155mm/52 calibre | Platform: 6x6 wheeled truck
- Range: 40km (ERFB-BB) / 50km (Vulcano) | ROF: 6 rpm | Weight: 18 tonnes (much lighter than tracked)
- Advantage: Much faster strategic mobility than tracked SPH; lower cost; simpler logistics
- Cost: ~$7M | Combat history: Afghanistan, Mali, Ukraine

### 2S19 Msta-S (Russia)
- Calibre: 152mm/47 calibre | Range: 29km (HE) / 36km (Krasnopol guided)
- Krasnopol: Laser-guided 152mm shell; CEP 1-2m; cost ~$20,000/round
- Export nations: Venezuela, Azerbaijan, Ethiopia

## Towed Artillery

### M777 Lightweight Howitzer (UK/USA)
- Calibre: 155mm/39 calibre | Range: 22km / 30km RAP / 40km Excalibur
- Weight: 4,218kg (titanium/aluminium — half weight of M198) | Crew: 5-8
- Airlift: Can be sling-loaded by CH-47/UH-60 — major tactical advantage
- Cost: ~$700K | Export: ITAR controlled; widely exported
- Combat history: Afghanistan, Ukraine (2022 — high impact)

### D-30 122mm Howitzer (USSR — 1963)
- Range: 15.4km | ROF: 7-8 rpm | Weight: 3.2 tonnes
- Still the most widely deployed towed artillery in Africa
- CPLP relevance: Angola, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau all operate D-30; ammunition procurement is a consistent market
- Cost: ~$100-200K (surplus)

### G6 Rhino (South Africa)
- Calibre: 155mm/45 calibre | Range: 30km+ | Wheeled (6x6) | South African designed and manufactured
- Exported to: UAE, Oman, Malaysia
- ARIA note: South African platform — relevant for CPLP market procurement as no ITAR applies; Denel supply chain

## Multiple Launch Rocket Systems (MLRS)

### BM-21 Grad (USSR — 1963)
- Rockets: 40 x 122mm unguided | Range: 20-40km depending on rocket type
- ROF: 40 rockets in 20 seconds (full ripple) | Weight: 13.7 tonnes | Cost: ~$300K per system
- CPLP relevance: Most common MLRS in Africa; Angola, Mozambique, Ethiopia, Sudan operate Grad
- Accuracy: Unguided — area saturation weapon; CEP ~200m at max range

### M270 MLRS / M142 HIMARS (USA)
- M270: Tracked; 12 x 227mm rockets or 2 x ATACMS ballistic missiles
- M142 HIMARS: Wheeled; 6 x 227mm or 1 x ATACMS
- GMLRS (Guided MLRS): 70km range; CEP 5-10m; GPS+INS
- ATACMS: 165-300km range | PrSM: 500km range (new)
- Combat history: Ukraine (2022) — HIMARS transformed the conflict; precision strikes on Russian logistics and command nodes
- Cost: M142 HIMARS ~$5.5M | GMLRS round ~$168,000 | ATACMS ~$1.5M
- Export: ITAR controlled; major political sensitivity

### Chinese MLRS (Polonez/Lynx/AR-3)
- Chinese-origin MLRS widely proliferated to Africa, Middle East
- Calibres: 300mm | Range: 130-290km (AR-3)
- Export: No ITAR/Wassenaar restriction
- ARIA note: China using MLRS sales as a geopolitical tool in Africa; understanding Chinese export terms relevant for CPLP market BD
