# ARIA Military Hardware — Air Defence Systems
Source: Arkmurus Hardware Library v1.0 | Classification: ARKMURUS INTERNAL

## ARIA Air Defence Doctrine

Air defence is a layered system — ARIA understands:
- LAYER 1: Point defence — MANPADS, short-range systems (<10km)
- LAYER 2: Short/medium range — IR/radar guided, 10-50km
- LAYER 3: Long range — S-300/400, Patriot (50-400km)

Ukraine conflict insight: Air defence density determines freedom of operation; layered defence essential.

## MANPADS (Man-Portable Air Defence Systems)

### 9K32 Strela-2 / SA-7 Grail (USSR — 1968)
- Range: 4.2km | Altitude: 2,300m | IR seeker (uncooled)
- Most widely proliferated MANPADS in history
- CPLP relevance: Extensively used in Angolan civil war; stockpiles remain; proliferation risk to non-state actors
- Export control: ML4 in Wassenaar; strict controls due to anti-civil-aviation threat

### 9K38 Igla (Needle) / SA-18 Grouse (USSR — 1983)
- Range: 5.2km | Altitude: 3,500m | Improved IR seeker
- Counters flares better than Strela | Still widely used in Africa, Middle East
- Cost: ~$50,000-80,000 per round

### FIM-92 Stinger (USA — 1981)
- Range: 4.8km | Altitude: 3,800m | Dual IR/UV seeker
- Significantly better IRCCM than Igla
- Combat history: Soviet-Afghan War (decisive), Gulf War, Iraq, Ukraine (2022)
- Export: ITAR controlled; sensitive — requires end-user certification
- Cost: ~$38,000-$120,000 per missile

### Mistral (France — 1988)
- Range: 6km | Altitude: 3,000m | Imaging IR seeker
- Export: French/EU licence; used in 25+ countries

## Short & Medium Range Air Defence

### ZU-23-2 (USSR — 1960)
- Twin 23mm autocannon | Range: 2.5km (effective) | No guided missile
- Ubiquitous in Africa — used on ground mounts, vehicles, technicals
- Cost: ~$20,000-50,000 | Zero export restrictions from most suppliers
- CPLP relevance: Every CPLP African military operates some ZU-23-2; ammunition procurement is consistent and significant

### ZSU-23-4 Shilka (USSR — 1965)
- 4 x 23mm autocannon on tracked chassis | Radar-guided
- Range: 2.5km | Highly effective anti-helicopter/low-level
- Still in service across Africa and Middle East

### Buk-M2/M3 (Russia) / SA-11/17
- Medium range: 50km | Altitude: 25,000m
- Proven effective against aircraft and cruise missiles
- Notoriety: MH17 shootdown (2014 — Buk used by Russia-backed forces)

### Crotale (France) / Sky Guard (Switzerland)
- Short range radar-guided | French system widely exported
- ARIA note: European alternative to Russian systems; relevant for African clients seeking to diversify from Russian supply

### Patriot PAC-3 (USA)
- Long-range: 160km+ | High altitude | Terminal phase hit-to-kill
- Cost: ~$4M per interceptor | Battery: ~$1B system cost
- Combat history: Gulf War, Iraq, Saudi Arabia vs Houthi missiles; Ukraine (2023)
- Export: ITAR controlled; only close allies
- ARIA note: Understanding Patriot procurement constraints is essential — one of the most politically sensitive US arms exports

### NASAMS (Norway/USA — Kongsberg/Raytheon)
- Medium range; uses AIM-120 AMRAAM as interceptor
- Ukraine supplied 2022: Proved highly effective (claimed 100% success rate against cruise missiles initially)
- Multiple nations now ordering; significant international demand

### Iron Dome (Israel)
- Point defence against rockets, artillery, mortars (C-RAM)
- Range: 70km | Cost per interceptor (Tamir): ~$50,000-80,000
- 90%+ success rate against rockets from Gaza (claimed)
- ARIA note: Growing interest from African states facing insurgent rocket threats — Mozambique Cabo Delgado context
